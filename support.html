Support

How to get help with the Miksumia Publisher application.

Write to us

Send your request to contact@miksumia.com.

What to include

  • The URL of your Confluence site.
  • What you did.
  • What you saw.
  • The time it happened.
  • A screenshot, if you can.

Never send the contents of your documents, nor any password or token.

Security issues

To report a vulnerability, follow our security policy.

Privacy
