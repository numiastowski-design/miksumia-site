Your Drive folders become real Confluence pages.

Miksumia Publisher reads a Google Drive folder and publishes its content in Confluence as native pages — searchable, open to comments, governed like the rest of your space. The folder tree becomes the page tree, and every change in Drive is resynchronised.

Pages, not attachments

Existing tools display the Drive file in an embedded frame: the content remains invisible to Confluence search and to agents. Here, every document is converted into native Confluence content — headings, lists, tables, images — with the original file always one click away.

A synchronisation that breaks nothing

After the first publication, only the documents that have actually changed are republished. Pages keep their identity, their version history and their incoming links. If a document is renamed or moved in Drive, the corresponding page follows without being recreated.

What the application reads, and what it does not write

  • Access to Google Drive is read-only. The application never modifies, moves or deletes a Drive file.
  • Only the folders you explicitly connect are read.
  • The application runs on Atlassian Forge: processing takes place within Atlassian's infrastructure, not on a third-party server.
  • Only a Confluence administrator can create, modify or delete a connection.

The application is currently distributed by invitation, through an installation link. Write to contact@miksumia.com to get access.
