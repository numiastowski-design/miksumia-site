Privacy policy

“Miksumia Publisher” application · Version of 5 October 2026

This policy describes the data processed by the Miksumia Publisher application (“the application”), published by Miksumia SARL (“we”), and how it is used, retained and deleted.

The application is an Atlassian Forge application installed by an administrator on a Confluence Cloud site. It reads files in Google Drive and publishes their content as Confluence pages.

For any question about this policy or about exercising your rights: contact@miksumia.com.

2. Google data the application accesses

The application requests three Google permissions, and only these:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly — read-only access to Google Drive. The application can neither create, nor modify, nor delete a file in Google Drive.
  • openid — technical OpenID Connect sign-in scope, required by Google to provide the email address below. It grants access to no content, and the account identifier it provides is neither read nor retained by the application.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email — the email address of the connected account, in order to display it to the administrator. It is read on demand and never retained.

Only the folders explicitly connected by a Confluence administrator are read. The rest of the connected account's Drive is never browsed.

Miksumia Publisher's use and transfer, to any other application, of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Miksumia Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, we use this data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above; we do not transfer it to third parties, except to provide these features, to comply with a legal obligation, or as part of a merger or an acquisition; we do not use it for advertising purposes; and no human reads it, except with your explicit consent for a support need, for security reasons, or where the law requires it.

Data obtained from Google APIs is not used to train generalised artificial intelligence models.

4. Where data is processed and stored

The application runs entirely on the Atlassian Forge platform. Processing and storage take place within Atlassian's infrastructure; we operate no server of our own and hold no copy of your documents outside this platform.

What we retain

  • Connection configuration: Drive folder identifier, destination Confluence space, synchronisation settings, exclusion rules. Retained as long as the connection exists.
  • Per-document synchronisation state: Drive file identifier, title, file type, parent folder, identifier of the Confluence page created, an irreversible cryptographic fingerprint (SHA-256) of the published content, as well as the technical indicators needed for tracking: revision number of the source, languages of a requested translation, and, in case of failure, a technical error code and message. These items make it possible to detect that a document has changed and to resume interrupted processing, without retaining the content of the document. Retained as long as the connection exists, then erased when it is deleted; without deletion, until uninstallation (section 7).
  • Execution logs: synchronisation dates, counters, technical identifiers and error codes. We never write document content, tokens or authentication credentials to them. An error message produced by a third-party library may, exceptionally, quote a fragment of the document that caused the failure; these logs are retained by the Atlassian Forge platform and are used by us only for diagnosis.
  • Text extracted from images (legacy): until 17 September 2026, text read from images was kept in a cache, indexed by the image's cryptographic fingerprint. The application no longer writes to it. The entries already present are not erased when a connection is deleted; they disappear with all of the storage upon uninstallation, under the conditions described in section 7.

What we do not retain

  • The full content of your documents is not stored durably: it is read, converted, published in Confluence, then discarded.
  • Google access and refresh tokens are kept in the encrypted secrets storage provided by Atlassian Forge. They are never logged or exposed to the interface.

Once published in Confluence, the content resides in your own Confluence space and is subject to your organisation's retention policy, as well as to Atlassian's terms.

5. What leaves the application, and why

The application has no server of its own: all processing and all storage are hosted by Atlassian. The only data that leaves this infrastructure is the data listed below.

Google Gemini — the only service that receives content

Two processes, and only two, send content belonging to you to the Google Gemini API. Both depend on a choice made by an administrator: AI reading is requested document by document; translation, once a language has been chosen for a folder, applies to every synchronisation of that folder.

  • AI reading of a scanned PDF: when an administrator has enabled AI reading of a scanned PDF, the application sends the pages of that document to Gemini to extract its text. Images contained in other documents are never sent.
  • Translation: when an administrator chooses a translation language for a folder, the text of each document (paragraphs, cells, headings) is sent to Gemini to be translated.
  • What is sent: for AI reading, the pages of the scanned PDF; for translation, the text of the document; in both cases, a fixed text instruction, identical on every call, which contains none of your data.
  • What is not sent: neither the document title, nor the Drive file name, nor the destination Confluence space, nor any information about the user.
  • What comes back: for AI reading, the text read, a short description and a reliability score; for translation, the translated text.
  • When this happens: only for a scanned PDF for which an administrator has enabled AI reading, and for the documents of a folder for which an administrator has chosen a translation language.

Google Drive and Google Sheets

These APIs receive the access token and the identifiers of the folders and files you have connected, and return the metadata and the content to convert. The Sheets API is used in addition to the Drive API because only it reveals the hidden ranges of a spreadsheet — and therefore makes it possible not to publish data you had chosen to hide.

Google authentication

The authentication service receives the authorisation code or the refresh token, as well as the application's credentials. No data belonging to you passes through it. On disconnection, the application calls token revocation with Google and deletes the token from its encrypted storage, whether or not Google responds.

Change notifications

To be informed that a document has changed, the application registers with Google a notification address and a verification token that it generated itself. No content passes through them.

What never leaves

  • No Confluence content. The flow is one-way: from Drive to Confluence.
  • No Atlassian user data: no name, no address, no account identifier.
  • No telemetry, no audience measurement, no external logging. The application sends statistics to no one.
  • No data to a server belonging to us: we operate none.

These services are used under their paid offerings, whose terms exclude the use of transmitted data for training models.

6. Legal basis and purpose

Processing is based on the performance of the service requested by your organisation: publishing in Confluence documents that you have explicitly designated to us. No data is used for advertising, profiling or resale purposes.

7. Retention and deletion of your data

  • Disconnecting a connection ends its synchronisation. The pages already published in Confluence are either detached — they stay in place and are no longer updated — or moved to the trash of your space, depending on the choice you make at the time of disconnection. The connection configuration and the synchronisation state are retained: they make it possible to resume an interrupted disconnection and to reconnect the folder later.
  • Deleting a connection, possible once it has been disconnected, erases the inventory of synchronised documents, the folder mappings, the exclusion rules and the connection itself.
  • Disconnecting the Google account revokes the authorisation and deletes the corresponding tokens. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions.
  • Uninstalling the application first revokes the application's Google authorisation, then triggers the deletion, by Atlassian, of all data stored by the application on your site. This deletion is not instantaneous: Atlassian first performs a soft deletion, then retains the data for 28 days before it is permanently erased. During the first 21 days, and only at your request, this data can be attached to a reinstallation of the application; after that, it can no longer be recovered. This period is that of the Atlassian Forge platform, which hosts the application, and not a choice on our part (Forge hosted storage data lifecycle).
  • You can at any time request the deletion of data concerning you by writing to contact@miksumia.com. We respond within 30 days.

8. Security

  • Exchanges with Google and Atlassian take place exclusively over HTTPS.
  • Secrets and tokens are stored in Forge's encrypted secrets store, never in plain text in the code or in the logs.
  • Access to the application's configuration is restricted to Confluence administrators, verified on every request.
  • The application requests the minimum scope of permissions needed for it to function.

9. Your rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have a right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection regarding data concerning you. Send your request to contact@miksumia.com: the application offers no self-service export, and we respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

10. Changes

Any substantial change to this policy will be published on this page, with an updated version date. We inform the administrators of the sites where the application is installed when the change affects the data processed.
