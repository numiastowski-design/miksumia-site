Privacy policy

“Miksumia Publisher” application · Version of 8 November 2026

This policy describes the data processed by the Miksumia Publisher application (“the application”), published by Miksumia SARL (“we”), and how it is used, retained and deleted.

The application is an Atlassian Forge application installed by an administrator on a Confluence Cloud site. A user uploads files by hand (Word, PowerPoint, Excel, PDF, images), and the application publishes each one as a Confluence page.

1. Data controller and contact

MIKSUMIA, société à responsabilité limitée (limited liability company), SIREN 919 860 452, whose registered office is at 70 boulevard Desgranges, 92330 Sceaux, France.

For any question about this policy or about exercising your rights: contact@miksumia.com.

For the content of the documents published through the application, the customer is the data controller and MIKSUMIA the processor (terms of use, Provider-Specific Term 5).

2. Data the application processes

DataWhy it is neededHow long it is kept

The result of an upload: the uploader's Atlassian account identifier, the destination page identifier, the identifier and title of the page created, our status and error codes Show the uploader, in the application's screen, what was published and what failed Erased when the screen has shown it; 24 hours at the most

The application reads only the files a user chooses to upload. It reads no other Confluence content than what it needs to check the uploader's permission and to show the spaces, pages and folders they may choose.

3. Where data is processed and stored

The application runs entirely on the Atlassian Forge platform. Processing and storage take place within Atlassian's infrastructure; we operate no server of our own and hold no copy of your documents outside this platform.

What we do not retain

  • The content of your documents: it is read, converted, published in Confluence, then discarded.
  • No record of which document was uploaded, by whom or when, beyond the 24-hour result above.
  • No file name and no document content in the logs. The logs hold only our error codes and technical identifiers.

An erased file stays restorable by Atlassian for 21 days, on a support request only, and is invisible to everyone else meanwhile. This period is that of the Atlassian Forge platform, not a choice on our part.

Once published in Confluence, the content resides in your own Confluence space and is subject to your organisation's retention policy, as well as to Atlassian's terms.

4. What leaves Atlassian

Nothing. The application makes no network call outside Atlassian: no AI service, no telemetry, no audience measurement, no external logging, and no server of ours. It sends statistics to no one.

5. Legal basis and purpose

Processing is based on the performance of the service requested by your organisation: publishing in Confluence the documents a user uploads. No data is used for advertising, profiling, model training or resale.

6. Retention and deletion of your data

  • Published pages belong to your Confluence site. The application never updates or deletes them; you manage them like any other page.
  • Uninstalling the application triggers the deletion, by Atlassian, of all data stored by the application on your site. This deletion is not instantaneous: Atlassian first performs a soft deletion, then retains the data for 28 days before it is permanently erased. During the first 21 days, and only at your request, this data can be attached to a reinstallation of the application; after that, it can no longer be recovered. This period is that of the Atlassian Forge platform, and not a choice on our part (Forge hosted storage data lifecycle).
  • You can at any time request the deletion of data concerning you by writing to contact@miksumia.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Security

  • Exchanges with Atlassian take place exclusively over HTTPS.
  • Before anything is published, the application checks, with the uploader's own Confluence rights, that they may create a page at the chosen place.
  • The application requests only the Confluence permissions it needs.

8. Your rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have a right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection regarding data concerning you. Send your request to contact@miksumia.com: the application offers no self-service export, and we respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

9. Changes

Any substantial change to this policy will be published on this page, with an updated version date. We inform the administrators of the sites where the application is installed when the change affects the data processed.
